AML & KYC Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) Policy explains the controls Nafini operates, in cooperation with our regulated partner banks, to prevent financial crime.

1. Our commitment

Nafini complies with the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, the CBN AML/CFT/CPF Regulations 2022, the Terrorism (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, and standards set by the Nigeria Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU). Nafini is registered with the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) where applicable through our partner institutions.

2. Customer identification

  • We apply tiered Customer Due Diligence (CDD) at sign-up — verifying WhatsApp number, business details, and (where applicable) BVN, NIN, and corporate registration (CAC) through partner institutions, in line with the CBN three-tiered KYC framework.
  • We apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for higher-value transactions, Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), and higher-risk customer profiles, in line with CBN AML/CFT Regulations 2022, Section 6.
  • We refuse onboarding where identification cannot be reasonably verified.

3. Monitoring

Transaction patterns are monitored for unusual activity such as structuring, rapid in-and-out flows, or other red flags consistent with money laundering or terrorism financing.

4. Reporting

Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) and Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) are filed with the Nigeria Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) within 24 hours of detection, in compliance with the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022.

5. Record keeping

We retain KYC records and transaction logs for a minimum of 5 years after the end of the relationship, in line with Section 7 of the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 and NFIU requirements.

6. Sanctions screening

Users and counterparties are screened against applicable sanctions lists before onboarding and on an ongoing basis.

7. Training

All Nafini staff undergo periodic AML/CFT training. A designated Chief Compliance Officer (CCO), independent of business lines, oversees the programme and reports directly to senior management, in line with the CBN AML/CFT Regulations 2022.
