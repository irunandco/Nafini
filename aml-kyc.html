AML & KYC Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) Policy explains the controls Nafini operates, in cooperation with our regulated partner banks, to prevent financial crime.

1. Our commitment

Nafini complies with the Nigeria Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, the CBN AML/CFT Regulations, and the standards of our partner financial institutions.

2. Customer identification

  • We verify each user's identity at sign-up via WhatsApp number, business details, and (where applicable) BVN/NIN through partner institutions.
  • We may request additional documentation for higher-value or higher-risk transactions.
  • We refuse onboarding where identification cannot be reasonably verified.

3. Monitoring

Transaction patterns are monitored for unusual activity such as structuring, rapid in-and-out flows, or other red flags consistent with money laundering or terrorism financing.

4. Reporting

Suspicious activity reports are filed with the Nigeria Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU) and partner banks as required by law.

5. Record keeping

We retain KYC records and transaction logs for a minimum of 5 years after the end of the relationship, in line with NFIU requirements.

6. Sanctions screening

Users and counterparties are screened against applicable sanctions lists before onboarding and on an ongoing basis.

7. Training

All Nafini staff undergo periodic AML/KYC training. A designated compliance officer oversees the programme.
