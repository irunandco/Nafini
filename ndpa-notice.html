NDPA Privacy Notice

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023: Nafini processes personal data in line with the NDPA and the supervisory authority's regulations. This notice summarises your rights as a Nigerian data subject.

1. Data controller

Nafini is the data controller responsible for personal data collected through our Service.

2. Lawful basis for processing

  • Consent — when you sign up and opt in to features.
  • Contract — to provide the tools and services you request.
  • Legitimate interest — to keep our Service secure, prevent fraud, and improve features.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with Nigerian laws, including KYC and tax rules.

3. Your rights under NDPA

  • Right to access your data.
  • Right to rectify inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (where applicable).
  • Right to restrict or object to processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

4. International transfers

Where data is processed outside Nigeria, we use providers with adequate safeguards in line with NDPA standards.

5. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Contact our DPO on WhatsApp: +234 815 202 9380. Email correspondence can be sent through our contact page.
