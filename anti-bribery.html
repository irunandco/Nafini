Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Nafini does not tolerate bribery or corruption in any form. This policy applies to all directors, staff, contractors, agents, and partners acting on our behalf.

1. Zero tolerance

We prohibit offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting anything of value with the intent of improperly influencing a business decision, regulator, customer, or counterparty.

2. Gifts and hospitality

Modest, transparent business hospitality (e.g. a meal at a meeting) is acceptable. Cash, cash equivalents, or anything that could be seen as influencing a decision is not.

3. Facilitation payments

Facilitation payments — small bribes to speed up routine action — are prohibited under all circumstances.

4. Third parties

Partners, vendors, and intermediaries acting for Nafini must comply with this policy. Breaches may result in termination of the relationship.

5. Record-keeping

All payments are recorded accurately. We do not maintain off-the-book accounts or false documentation.

6. Reporting

If you become aware of suspected bribery or corruption, report it via our Whistleblower channel or WhatsApp: +234 815 202 9380. We protect good-faith reporters from retaliation.

7. Compliance

This policy aligns with the Corrupt Practices and Other Related Offences Act, the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act, and international standards (UK Bribery Act / FCPA) where relevant to our partners.
