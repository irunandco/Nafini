Information Security Statement

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This is a public summary of how Nafini protects customer information. The detailed internal policy is reviewed annually by management.

1. Principles

  • Confidentiality — data is only accessible to authorised people who need it.
  • Integrity — data is protected from unauthorised changes.
  • Availability — the Service is designed to remain available when you need it.

2. Technical safeguards

  • End-to-end encryption on all WhatsApp conversations (provided by WhatsApp).
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for stored data.
  • Role-based access control with least-privilege principles.
  • Multi-factor authentication on internal admin systems.
  • Regular vulnerability scanning and patching.

3. Operational safeguards

  • Background checks on staff with access to sensitive systems.
  • Annual security and data-protection training.
  • Vendors are reviewed for security posture before integration.

4. Incident response

We maintain a documented incident response plan. In the event of a material breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected users and the NDPC within the timelines required by the NDPA.

5. Reporting a security concern

Found a vulnerability or security issue? Please report it responsibly to +234 815 202 9380. We will not pursue good-faith researchers who comply with this policy.
