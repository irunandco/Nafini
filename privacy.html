Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nafini ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our WhatsApp-based services, website, and any related tools (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account & contact info — your name, WhatsApp number, and business name when you sign up.
  • Business activity — transactions, expenses, sales summaries, and uploaded bank statements you share with us so we can prepare reports and recommendations.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical information (device type, country, time of access) to keep the Service reliable and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the features you ask for (bookkeeping, reports, reminders, tools).
  • To build your Nafini Trust Score and surface partner loan offers — only when you opt in.
  • To improve and secure the Service.
  • To send you product updates and support messages on WhatsApp.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your data. We share only with:

  • Regulated CBN-licensed partner banks that hold or move funds on your behalf.
  • Lending partners — only when you apply for a loan and only with your explicit consent.
  • Service providers who help us operate (hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality.
  • Authorities, where legally required.

4. WhatsApp end-to-end encryption

Your conversations with the Nafini assistant inside WhatsApp are protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption. Nafini can only see messages exchanged inside your dedicated Nafini chat.

5. Data retention

We retain your business records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal and accounting obligations. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Your rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent or delete your account.
  • Object to direct marketing.

7. Contact

Questions? Reach us on WhatsApp at +234 815 202 9380 or via our contact page.
