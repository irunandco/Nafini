Complaints & Dispute Resolution

Last updated: 16 May 2026

We aim to resolve every complaint fairly and quickly. This policy explains how to reach us and what to expect.

1. How to lodge a complaint

  • WhatsApp (fastest): +234 815 202 9380
  • Contact form: our contact page

2. What to include

  • Your name and WhatsApp number.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Relevant dates, transaction IDs, or screenshots.

3. Our response times

  • Acknowledgement: within 1 business day.
  • Initial resolution or update: within 5 business days.
  • Final response: within 15 business days for standard complaints.

4. Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may escalate to:

  • The Central Bank of Nigeria Consumer Protection Department — for payment-related disputes that involve a partner bank.
  • The Nigeria Data Protection Commission — for data protection complaints.
  • The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC) — for consumer issues.

5. Record keeping

We keep records of complaints and resolutions for at least 3 years, in line with regulatory expectations.
