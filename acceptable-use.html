Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy sets out how you may and may not use the Nafini Service. Breaches may result in suspension or termination of your account.

1. You may not

  • Use Nafini to facilitate fraud, money laundering, or financing of illegal activity.
  • Impersonate another person or business, or submit false KYC information.
  • Upload bank statements or documents that do not belong to you or your business.
  • Use Nafini to harass, threaten, defame, or send abusive content to anyone.
  • Resell, copy, or reverse-engineer the Service.
  • Attempt to disrupt, overload, or gain unauthorised access to our systems.

2. You must

  • Keep your WhatsApp number and credentials secure.
  • Provide accurate information about your business.
  • Comply with all applicable Nigerian laws — including tax, anti-money-laundering, and consumer protection rules.

3. Reporting abuse

If you encounter content or activity that breaches this policy, report it on WhatsApp: +234 815 202 9380.

4. Enforcement

We may suspend, restrict, or close accounts that breach this policy, and report serious breaches to law enforcement and partner banks where required.
