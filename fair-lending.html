Fair Lending Statement

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Nafini is committed to fair, transparent, and inclusive access to credit for small businesses in Nigeria. This statement clarifies our role and the standards our partner lenders are expected to uphold.

1. Nafini's role

Nafini is not a lender. We are a technology platform that helps small businesses keep records and — with explicit consent — connects them to regulated, licensed lenders.

2. Equal treatment

We do not discriminate, and we expect our partners not to discriminate, on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, age, marital status, or location, except where required by applicable law.

3. Transparent pricing

Partner lenders are required to disclose, clearly and upfront:

  • The full interest rate (and any fees) on an Effective Annual Rate basis.
  • Repayment schedule and total amount payable.
  • Penalties for late payment.
  • Consequences of default.

4. Responsible underwriting

Partners assess affordability before approving any loan and may decline applications where repayment would cause financial harm.

5. Collection practices

Collections must comply with CBN guidelines on ethical debt recovery. Harassment, threats, or contacting people not listed as references is strictly prohibited.

6. Complaints about a loan

If you have a complaint about a loan offered through Nafini, contact the partner lender directly. If unresolved, you can escalate via our Complaints & Dispute Resolution process or to the CBN Consumer Protection Department.
