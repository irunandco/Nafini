Whistleblower Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Nafini encourages anyone — staff, customers, partners, suppliers — to raise concerns about suspected wrongdoing inside the company without fear of retaliation.

1. What to report

  • Fraud, theft, or financial misconduct.
  • Bribery, corruption, or facilitation payments.
  • Breaches of laws or regulations.
  • Unsafe practices or risks to customer data.
  • Serious breaches of internal policies.

2. How to report

  • WhatsApp (confidential): +234 815 202 9380
  • Contact form: our contact page — mark "Whistleblower" in the subject.

You may report anonymously, although providing your name helps us investigate effectively.

3. Protection from retaliation

Anyone who reports a concern in good faith is protected from retaliation, including dismissal, demotion, harassment, or discrimination. Retaliation is treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

4. Handling reports

  • Reports are reviewed by a designated officer independent of the matter.
  • Confidentiality is maintained as far as possible.
  • We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days and provide an outcome update within 30 days.

5. False reports

Knowingly false or malicious reports are a breach of policy and may result in disciplinary action.
