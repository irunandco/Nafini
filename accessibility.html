Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Nafini is built to be usable by everyone — including users with disabilities, low-end devices, or unstable internet.

1. Our commitments

  • Operate via WhatsApp, the most accessible messaging app in Nigeria.
  • Keep the website lightweight so it works on slow connections.
  • Aim for WCAG 2.1 Level AA where applicable.
  • Use clear, plain English. Avoid jargon.

2. Features that help

  • Sufficient contrast and large tappable targets on mobile.
  • Keyboard-navigable interactive elements.
  • Alternative text on key images.
  • Lightweight pages that load on 2G networks.

3. Known limitations

Some embedded media (video, animated GIFs) may not have full text equivalents yet. We are working on adding captions and audio descriptions where appropriate.

4. Tell us

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on Nafini, please report it on WhatsApp: +234 815 202 9380. We aim to respond within 5 business days and resolve where possible.
