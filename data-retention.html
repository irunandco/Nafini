Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This policy explains how long Nafini keeps personal and business data, and how you can request deletion.

1. Retention periods

  • Account profile — for as long as your account is active, plus 12 months after closure.
  • Transaction & report data — minimum 7 years, to meet tax and accounting record-keeping obligations.
  • KYC records — minimum 5 years after the end of the relationship, per NFIU rules.
  • Chat history — stored only as long as needed to operate the Service and resolve disputes.
  • Marketing preferences — until you withdraw consent.

2. Right to deletion

You can request deletion of your account at any time. We will delete data that is not required for legal or regulatory reasons. Data retained for compliance is held securely and used only for those purposes.

3. Backups

Encrypted backups may retain deleted data for up to 90 days before they cycle out, after which it is permanently overwritten.

4. How to request deletion

Message us on WhatsApp: +234 815 202 9380. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.
