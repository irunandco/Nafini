Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Refund Policy applies to all paid tools and services purchased on Nafini (the "Service"). By making a payment, you agree to this policy.

1. Eligible refunds

We refund the full amount paid in the following cases:

  • Failed delivery — you paid for a tool (e.g. business plan, P&L report, pitch deck) but did not receive the output within the stated turnaround time, and the delay was caused by Nafini.
  • Duplicate charge — you were billed more than once for the same tool.
  • Service unavailable — the tool could not be generated due to a technical issue on our end that we could not resolve within 72 hours.

2. Non-refundable cases

We are unable to issue refunds when:

  • The tool has already been delivered and was generated based on the information you provided.
  • The output is rejected for reasons related to information you supplied (e.g. wrong figures, incomplete inputs) — we will offer one free re-run instead.
  • The request involves third-party fees or partner-bank charges that have already been remitted.
  • Refund is requested more than 7 days after delivery.

3. How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp at +234 815 202 9380 with:

  • Your name and WhatsApp number used at purchase.
  • The tool you paid for and the date of payment.
  • A brief explanation of the issue.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Processing speed may vary depending on your bank or card issuer.

5. Loans and partner products

Nafini does not process refunds for loans, repayments, or any product offered by an independent partner bank or lender. For those, please reach out to the partner directly.

6. Contact

Questions about a refund? WhatsApp us: +234 815 202 9380.
