Frequently
Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions about how Nafini works.

Watch explainer
2 min overview
Do I need to download a new app?
+
No. Nafini lives entirely inside WhatsApp. You use it just like you are texting a friend.
Can Nafini see my personal WhatsApp chats?
+
Never. Nafini can only see and reply to messages inside your private, dedicated chat window with the Nafini assistant.
What happens if I make zero sales this month?
+
You pay ₦0. There are no monthly subscriptions or hidden maintenance fees. We only take a tiny success fee when you successfully process a sale.
How do I get a loan through Nafini?
+
You don't need paperwork. By using Nafini consistently for 30 to 90 days, you automatically build a Nafini Trust Score. If your score is healthy, Nafini unlocks pre-approved growth loans right inside your chat.
Is my money safe with Nafini?
+
Yes. Your funds are held in a secure, CBN-licensed partner bank — not by Nafini. Every transaction is end-to-end encrypted and you can withdraw or move your money at any time.
Is my data private and protected?
+
Always. Your chats are encrypted by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption. Nafini never sells your data, never shares it with third parties, and only uses it to run the services you ask for.
What if someone gets access to my phone?
+
You can enable WhatsApp's built-in Chat Lock on your Nafini conversation — it requires Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN every time the chat is opened, so even someone holding your unlocked phone can't see it. On top of that, every sensitive action (payouts, transfers, withdrawals) needs a one-time PIN you set up. If your phone is ever lost or stolen, message us from any other device to freeze your account instantly.
