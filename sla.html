Service Level Agreement

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) describes the availability and support commitments Nafini makes to users of paid tools.

1. Availability target

We target 99.5% monthly uptime for the Nafini WhatsApp assistant and web tools, in line with the CBN Electronic Payments and Collections Framework and industry standards for Nigerian fintech operators. Scheduled maintenance, force majeure, and downstream provider outages (e.g. WhatsApp, partner banks) are excluded.

2. Support response

  • Urgent (Service down, payment failed, suspected fraud): response within 2 hours, 24/7. Failed transactions are reversed within 72 hours per CBN regulations.
  • High (paid tool delivery): response within 1 business day.
  • Normal (general questions): response within 2 business days.

3. Paid tool delivery

  • Generator tools (Business Plan, Pitch Deck): instant after submission.
  • Done-for-you reports (P&L, Cash Flow): within 24 hours of submission.

4. If we miss the SLA

If we fail to deliver a paid tool within the stated timeline due to our fault, you are entitled to a full refund per the Refund Policy or a free re-run, at your choice.

5. Business hours

Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 West Africa Time, excluding public holidays.

6. Contact

Reach our support team: +234 815 202 9380.
